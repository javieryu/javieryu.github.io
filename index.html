Javier Yu

I am a research scientist working at Toyota Research Institute on autonomous driving.

Before TRI, I completed a Ph.D. in Aeronautics and Astronautics at Stanford University. I was advised by Professor Mac Schwager as part of the Multi-robot Systems Lab and was funded on an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship.

As a roboticist I'm interested in practical solutions for problems at the intersection of learning and perception. Recently, my work has focused on architecture design, post-training, and deployment of vision-based models for autonomous driving.
